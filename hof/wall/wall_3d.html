How to Explore the Hall of Fame

Look
Click and drag to look around · scroll to zoom · I / K look up / down

Jump
Double-click anywhere on the concourse floor to walk there · click the minimap · use Explore Views in the top bar
Plaques
Click any inductee plaque on the wall to read their full biography, stats, and Hall of Fame class
Back
Backspace or ↶ returns to the previous camera angle · H or ⌂ returns to the main overview
